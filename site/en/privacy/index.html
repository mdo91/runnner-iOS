What stays on your iPhone

Runner reads running workouts and the measurements you allow through Apple Health: dates, distance, duration, heart rate, energy, routes, running dynamics, and available VO₂ max or recovery measurements. Local processing does not upload these records to Runner. Apple controls Health permissions, and does not tell Runner which read permissions you denied.

The local database is protected by iOS file protection and excluded from device backups. Locally saved reports remain until you clear the app’s data or remove the app. Clearing Runner data does not delete workouts in Apple Health.

Photos and videos you select are edited on the device. Runner does not upload them to its backend or AI provider. Exports omit source location metadata, but any route or statistics you visibly place in an image or video will be visible to its recipients. A service you choose in the share sheet processes the shared file under its own policy.

Optional accounts and AI analysis

Measured statistics do not require an account. Optional cloud features use Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication. Runner does not request your Apple name or email scopes; Apple and Firebase process the identity credentials and account information they provide for authentication. Runner uses a Firebase user identifier to associate cloud records with your account.

AI analysis requires sign-in, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and an explicit separate consent. It sends numerical run summaries, splits, data-quality flags, and a compact baseline to the Runner service and Google Gemini. It does not send precise routes, raw HealthKit samples, names, email addresses, or your Firebase user identifier to Gemini.

After consent, the latest run and newly imported runs may be analyzed automatically. Older runs and regeneration in another language are requested explicitly. AI is fitness coaching, not diagnosis or treatment. You can turn it off in Settings. Existing saved reports are not automatically erased by turning AI off.

Optional history, routes, and dashboard

Cloud history and precise GPS routes are separate choices and start off. With history sync enabled, Runner stores measured run history, splits, chart summaries, dated endurance measurements, and saved reports in your account. Routes are included only if you also enable GPS sync; they are never sent to Gemini.

A browser dashboard requires a code that you approve in the iPhone app. Its session is read-only, lasts at most 12 hours, and ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. Link codes expire after 10 minutes. Short-lived browser and network security records prevent abuse.

Turning history sync off stops new uploads and removes uploaded routes, while numerical history remains until you delete it. Turning GPS sync off removes uploaded coordinates across the account. If your iPhone is offline, it finishes that request when it reconnects.

Technical data and service providers

Firebase initializes when the app starts, including before optional cloud consent. Firebase Authentication collects technical SDK/version diagnostics for service analytics. Authentication and App Attest process credentials, attestation information, and security signals. These are not advertising tracking.

Runner uses Google Firebase Authentication, App Check, Cloud Run, and Firestore. Our Cloud Run service and Firestore database are in europe-west1; this does not mean all provider processing stays in the EU. Firebase Authentication operates in US data centers. Google Gemini processes the numerical inputs described above. Cloud infrastructure records request metadata, including IP addresses, user agents, URLs, status, and timing, for operation and security. We do not put workout payloads or authentication tokens in application logs.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, contact forms, or marketing cookies. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

We do not sell Health data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Provider processing follows their applicable terms and safeguards. See Firebase privacy information and Gemini terms.

Retention and deletion

Runner’s completed-analysis cache expires after 24 hours. Quota records expire after two days. Firestore’s physical TTL deletion is asynchronous, so expiry is not a promise of immediate physical erasure. Synced history has no automatic expiry and stays until you delete it. The dashboard uses short-lived session, link, and rate-limit records. Minimal deletion markers prevent delayed requests from restoring deleted account or history data.

Our standard Cloud Run log bucket currently retains request logs for 30 days. Firebase reports retaining authentication security IP logs for weeks and removing other deleted authentication information from live and backup systems within 180 days. These provider/security periods are separate from the app’s history and cache controls.

The Gemini service is used with a billed project. Google’s paid-service terms state that prompts and outputs are not used to improve its products. This does not mean zero retention: Google’s abuse-monitoring policy describes retention of prompts, context, and output for 55 days and authorized review of flagged material. Provider obligations, security investigations, and backups can have separate retention rules.

Your controls and rights

In Settings → Your data, you can clear local data, delete uploaded history, or delete your cloud account. Account deletion requires Apple confirmation and removes synced history/routes, Runner analysis cache, dashboard access, and the Firebase account. It does not delete Apple Health workouts. You can also revoke Health permissions in Apple’s Health or Settings app.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent for future processing without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Contact mahmutaoata@gmail.com; please do not email raw Health records unless we specifically arrange a secure way to receive them.

For EU users, optional Health/fitness uploads and precise-route processing rely on consent, including explicit consent where special-category data is involved (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Requested account services rely on performance of the service; proportionate security and technical operation rely on legitimate interests (Articles 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f)). International provider transfers use applicable adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority, including the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.
