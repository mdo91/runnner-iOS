Contact

Email mahmutaoata@gmail.com. Tell us your iOS version, Runner version, and what happened. Please avoid sending personal Health records, precise routes, passwords, or sign-in codes.

My runs are missing

Check Runner’s read access in Apple Health and allow time for Apple Watch data to sync. Runner imports completed running workouts; it does not manufacture missing measurements. Pull down to refresh. Apple does not reveal denied read permissions to the app.

AI or cloud history is unavailable

Measured statistics work without an account. AI requires Sign in with Apple, adult confirmation, a separate consent, an internet connection, and available quota (up to three completed-run analyses per day). Cloud history and precise GPS require their own choices. You can keep using local measurements when cloud services are unavailable.

Photos and videos

Choose media from your device, position your overlays, and use Save or Share. Saving requires permission to add to Photos. If media is stored in iCloud, allow it to download. A shared route can reveal where you run; hide it if you do not want to share that location.

Delete data or withdraw consent

Open Settings → Your data to clear local records, delete uploaded history, or delete your cloud account. Disable AI, history sync, or GPS separately in Settings. Offline GPS-removal requests finish when you reconnect. Read the privacy policy for retention and provider details.

Availability

Runner 1.0 is being prepared for the App Store. It is designed for iPhone with iOS 26 or later, in English and German. The app language follows your iPhone’s app-language setting. No Runner Watch companion is included in this release; Apple Watch workouts recorded in Apple Health can still be imported.
